Wet asphalt with faint white parking lines reflects the gray sky. The car sits in the lower third, seen from a low three-quarter front angle, windows closed, a few warm interior lights on. No people. Muted, cool color palette, realistic, cinematic, 35mm lens, natural light, high detail.
```
