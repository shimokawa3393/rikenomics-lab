[Cosmic Scale Series #3] The Spiral of the Solar System

Textbooks show the planets circling the Sun on the same loops, over and over.

But the Sun itself is racing through the Milky Way at about 220 km/s.

Redraw it with the galaxy as the reference, and the planets' paths aren't closed circles at all. They're gentle spirals stretching on forever. After a year, Earth is back at the same point in its orbit, but in space it's somewhere completely new.

The feeling of "going around" only exists relative to something we assume is standing still. Nothing in the universe truly is.

*Orbital periods and the Sun's speed are real values. Only the spacing between orbits is schematic (evenly spaced).
