[Title]
Earth never returns to where it was last year #Shorts

[Description]
Textbooks show planets circling the Sun on the same loops. But the Sun itself is racing through the Milky Way at about 220 km/s.

In one year, that's about 7 billion km, roughly 1.5x the distance from the Sun to Neptune. Redraw it with the galaxy as the reference, and the orbits aren't closed circles. They're spirals stretching on forever.

Nothing in the universe is truly standing still.
