[Description]
The cars at the front only slow from 61 to 45 km/h. But each car behind brakes a little harder, and the next one harder still, until the cars further back crawl along below 20 km/h.

The cars keep moving forward, yet the jam grows backward at about 19 km/h. On real highways, jams are observed traveling backward at about 20 km/h.

Leave a gap and slow down early and gently. That alone can weaken the wave traveling backward.
