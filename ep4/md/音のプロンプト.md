# ep4 音の生成プロンプト

## 走行音（0〜4秒、4〜7秒でBGMと入れ替え）

映像: 夜のブルーアワー、日本の高速道路（片側3車線・乾いた路面・ナトリウム灯）。カメラは路肩の少し上（高さ約7m）から望遠で道路の先を見ている。
こちらの車線の車は遠ざかり、反対側の車線の車がときどき近くを通り過ぎる。まだ渋滞していない（時速約60kmで流れている）。
生成の長さは10秒前後。良い4〜7秒ぶんを切り出す。

### 本命（2回目以降）→ 採用（ユーザー「走行音は完璧」。5.5〜9.5秒の一定の区間を使う）
1回目の本命は2回とも「特定の車が通過するドップラー音」になった。通過を連想させる語(passes, whoosh, rumbles past)を抜き、
1台1台が聞き分けられない一定の背景音だと書く。
```
Constant highway traffic noise as a steady background bed. A dense, even wash of tire noise from hundreds of cars far away, blended into one smooth continuous roar. No single vehicle can be heard on its own. The sound stays at the same level from start to finish, like a uniform drone. Night, open air, wide stereo. No horns, no voices, no music.
```

### 1回目の本命（通過音になった）
```
Night highway ambience recorded from an overpass beside a multi-lane expressway. Steady, continuous tire roar on dry asphalt from many cars moving at moderate speed. Every few seconds a car passes close by with a soft whoosh, and one heavy truck rumbles past. Calm, wide, slightly distant stereo field. No horns, no voices, no sirens, no music.
```

### 近め（車の通過を強めたい）
```
Close roadside recording of cars passing on a highway at night, dry road. Clear individual pass-bys with smooth doppler whooshes, layered over a constant low traffic hum. Clean, realistic, no horns, no voices.
```

### 遠め（静かな入りにしたい）
```
Distant highway traffic at night, a low continuous rumble of tires carried across open ground, occasional faint truck passing. Quiet, spacious, realistic field recording. No horns, no voices, no insects.
```

## BGM（映像の0秒から走行音の下で小さく鳴らし、4〜7秒で大きくする。曲の秒数＝映像の秒数）

### 1曲目（不採用: 盛り上がらない）
```
Minimal electronic, instrumental, no vocals. 110 BPM.
Starts with a soft synth pulse and a clean arpeggio, steady and precise like data.
Layers are added one by one: a muted kick, a low bass line, a second arpeggio slightly out of phase.
Tension builds gradually without a big drop. Night city, cold and clear, slightly hypnotic.
Ends unresolved, fading on a sustained chord.
```
解析: 110BPMは指定どおり。0〜26秒の音量がずっと一定(-16dB)で、19〜23秒はむしろ高音が減る。「層を足す」と書いただけで、
いつ・どれだけ盛り上がるかを書かなかった。「without a big drop」「fading」も盛り上がりを弱める方向に効いた可能性。

### 2曲目（展開を秒数で指定）
```
Instrumental electronic track, exactly 30 seconds, 110 BPM, no vocals. Cold, precise, night-city mood, steadily escalating.
0-7s: Sparse and quiet. A single soft synth pulse on every beat, nothing else.
7-14s: A tight kick drum enters on every beat, plus a clean repeating arpeggio. Energy clearly rises.
14-22s: A deep driving bass line and closed hi-hats join. A filter on the arpeggio slowly opens, getting brighter and louder.
22-30s: Full intensity. A second higher arpeggio, a rising riser sweep, and the loudest, densest section of the track, still climbing.
At 30s, stop abruptly on the beat, with no fade-out and no resolution.
```
