No accident, no roadwork. So why the traffic jam? 🚗

The cars at the front only slow from 61 to 45 km/h.
But each car behind brakes a little harder, and the next one harder still, until they crawl below 20 km/h.

The cars keep moving forward, yet the jam grows backward at about 19 km/h. On real highways, jams are observed traveling backward at about 20 km/h.

Leave a gap and slow down early and gently. That alone can weaken the wave.

*Computed with a traffic-flow model (IDM, 1 s reaction delay): 30 cars per km per lane, 3 lanes. In this video, cars slow slightly on an uphill too gentle to notice.
*The video speeds up to 2x and 3x.
